What makes me, me?

I learned to think in signals: what is noise, what is filter, and what survives. At Qualcomm I chased power and thermal bugs for carmakers. Then I cofounded a hardware company and found out a clean signal only matters if someone can act on it.

Film and stage taught me how to tell that story. The MBA is teaching me to price it.
